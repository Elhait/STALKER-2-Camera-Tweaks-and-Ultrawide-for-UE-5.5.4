## Owner and gate

`runtime.cpp:156–355`: one private RuntimeState; one deliberate resident `*new RuntimeState{}` allocation; `cameraCorrectionGate` is its field (:168). The `g_*` aliases bind that owner or its HookSet members. There is no new callback-test RuntimeState implementation. Each fixture process uses this actual resident owner, then terminates; it does not copy/reassign mutex-bearing state.

Lock order remains correction gate → Dialogue mutex → Camera State mutex. Settings readback also takes gate → hotkey-config mutex. Log queues captured arguments under the gate and emits after release. Zoom has the existing owner-thread affinity contract and does **not** acquire the correction gate; V0 must not change this behavior.

Separate auxiliary storage remains separately identified: published RuntimeSettingsApi; notification mutex/queue/IDs; diagnostic function statics and TLS; current profile-gated observers. These are not alternative correction owners. HookSet macro-dependent members remain under its existing owner.
