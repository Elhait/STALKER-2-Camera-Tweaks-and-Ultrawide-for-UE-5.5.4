## Defect found by the generator

A second primary Writer ran after the first Writer proved the native provider endpoint, but before the setter consumed Dialogue admission. The first Writer corrected 70° to 134.282° at 21:9. The next Writer published 70°. This was reproduced from the independent graph without the recorded departure replay.

The endpoint-authorized mapping now keeps that frame corrected for the same receiver, thread, generation and settings epoch. This permission ends at admission or revocation. It preserves the current Gameplay reference and never creates Gameplay admission from the native Dialogue endpoint.

The graph covers a bounded callback domain. In particular it models role-confirming observations the existing native adapters can express. Required correlation still cannot be inferred from two identical scalar inputs alone.
